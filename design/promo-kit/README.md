# Laundromat — 20-second promo kit

Everything a video platform or an editor needs for the 20-second cut. Nothing
here needs to be looked up elsewhere.

- `promo-video-script.md` — the script: shot list, voiceover, timings.
- `frames/` — every frame, in order, named by the shot it belongs to.

## What these are

`01`–`05` are captured from the live site, https://play-laundromat.fly.dev, at
1920×1080 with a 2x device pixel ratio — so they are ~1700–3800px on the long
edge and will not soften when a platform pans or zooms across them.

`06-jimothy.png` is cut from the print master at 8x rather than taken from the
game, because the shipped card art is 248×400 and would have been the only soft
frame in the film. The die-cut lines are trimmed off.

## Two notes for whoever cuts it

**The whole film is one page.** The three mode cards sit at the top of the
landing page and the guide is the same page scrolled down, so this can be shot
as a single continuous scroll of the live site rather than assembled from these
stills. The stills are the fallback, and they are also what an image-to-video
platform will want.

**The Jimothy cut is the only one that matters.** The machine hum stops when he
appears and the frame goes still. He is the only living thing in the film and
the only shot with silence under it — that is what makes him the joke rather
than another rule.
