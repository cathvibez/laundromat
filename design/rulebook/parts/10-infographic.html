Silhouette, not just tone, so it survives bad light and colour-blindness. The dot's colour is who owns the card.

Shade is not owner colour. Every player's colour has dark cards and light cards. “Dark” is about how dirty the wash gets. Your colour is about who the card belongs to.

Two words worth learning now

Clothes — shoes, socks, pants, shirt, hat.

Linen — underwear and blanket.

A card of socks is a pair, and counts as one item. Most rules just say item, meaning any of the fourteen.

Provisional — damp markers
Damp is the only lasting per-item state in the game and the component list has no token for it. Current default: the face-up damp zone alone. The problem this leaves open: a damp sock sitting in a washer looks exactly like a dry one, and that is precisely when it matters.

A day

The game is played in days. Every day runs the same five steps.

1

Roll phase — everyone takes one turn

The keyholder goes first, then round to the left. Yesterday's keyholder goes last. The order rotates with the key.

each player's turn, in order — you may not go back
1–6
Roll the die

It tells you how many items you must load, and what else happens.

Play one card (optional)

At most one ready card, and only before you load. Never a card you drew today.

Load — mandatory

Put exactly as many items into washers as you rolled. Fewer only if you truly cannot.

Resolve the die's extra

The move on a 4, the card draw on a 5, the event on a 6.

Cards before laundry. The card comes first because the Mesh bag has to be open before anything goes in it. Everything else about the order follows from that.
2

Event — if one was drawn today provisional

Only the first 6 rolled each day draws an event card, and it is turned face up the instant it is drawn. At most one event per day.

3

Key — the keyholder switches one washer

One on, or one off, or nothing. Not both. Not two. The single most valuable action of the day.

4

Reckoning — every washer that is ON runs

This is where laundry gets washed. Washers that are off, destroyed, or occupied by Jimothy do not run at all. See the next five pages.

5

End of day

Fresh cards become ready. The key passes to the left. If anybody has ten clean items, they have already won.

Provisional — when a drawn event actually happens
Revealed on draw is settled. Resolved on draw is not. Three arms are live; the sequence above is written for the first.
  • E1 — immediate (as printed above). Fires mid-turn. Players yet to go play into the changed board. Only arm where a Coin or Snacc can answer it the same day. Cost: seat-order asymmetry.
  • E2 — deferred. Fires at day step 2, after everyone has loaded. Nothing can answer it.
  • E3 — split. Circuit break and Animal control on draw; Gang and Jimothy deferred.
This changes the numbered list above and nothing else in the booklet.

Loading

Put exactly as many of your items into washers as you rolled. They may go into the same washer or different ones. Loading is not optional.

A washer will take your item — unless one of these five

✕
It is full.
Four items is the limit, at every player count.
✕
It has been destroyed by the Gang.
Gone for good. Neither a source nor a destination for anything.
✕
Jimothy is in it.
A raccoon. Nothing goes in, nothing comes out.
✕
There is a blanket inside and your item is not socks.
✕
Your item is a blanket and the washer holds anything other than socks.

That is the whole list.

Blankets need a washer to themselves — except for socks

Legal ✓
Blanket
Socks may share with a blanket, in both directions.
Refused ✕
Blanket
A shirt cannot go in beside a blanket. Nothing but socks ever can.
Socks that wash beside a blanket come out DAMP. Not clean — one more wash, anywhere, finishes them. The rule keys on the blanket being in there, not on the blanket itself washing. See page 12.
A washer that is switched OFF can still be loaded. It just will not run, and it carries everything inside into tomorrow. This matters enormously after a Circuit break.

When you cannot load

You load fewer than you rolled only when:

  • your hand and damp zone together hold fewer items than that, or
  • no washer on the board will accept anything you hold.

Then you load as many as you legally can, which may be none. No penalty, no re-roll, no substitute action. Say so out loud so the table can see it, and move on. This is a real situation, not a technicality — after a Circuit break nothing drains and everyone keeps loading.
