The reckoning
Shoes first, then dark, then light. everything below is that line, spelled out

Every washer that is ON runs

Each one decides, item by item, what comes out clean and what comes back. Washers that are off, destroyed, or occupied by Jimothy do not run at all.

Nothing is ever lost

What washes goes to its owner's clean pile, permanently. Everything else goes straight back to its owner's hand, with no penalty of any kind. After a washer has run, it is empty.

One washer at a time

Washers never affect each other, so resolve them in any order. Order inside a washer never matters either.

Shoes beat everything, including other dark items. A's dark shoes, B's light shirt, C's dark pants → only A's shoes wash. C's dark pants are on rung 3 and lose to rung 1, even though they are dark.

Your own items never protect you. A's dark shirt, A's light shirt, B's dark shirt → A's light shirt sits on rung 4 under B's rung 3, so the room sends it back. Your own pile is judged separately, and never shields you from the room.

Crowding provisional — may be cut

If other players have put two or more items of the same type as yours into that washer — three of a type in total, counting yours — then yours is crowded out and comes back. Any colour, any shade; only the type matters. Your own copies never crowd you.

Three shoes cancel out. A's dark shoes, B's dark shoes, C's dark shoes and D's light shirt, exactly full → nothing washes at all. All three pairs clear the room, then crowding sends all three back; D's shirt is not rescued, because crowding sends items back and never promotes anything.

Coloring

If another player has played a Coloring card on the washer, your items there are ruined and come back — unless you have a Color catcher on that washer.

A's light shirt, B's light pants, C's light hat, with a Coloring played by A and a Color catcher played by B → A and B wash, C goes back. Everything is light, so the ladder would have washed all three. The catcher protects its owner and nobody else.

What the cards do to it

Three special item cards attach to a washer and are read when it runs.

Bleach

Swaps every shade in the washer before the room's ladder is read. It does not disarm shoes — bleached dark shoes are light shoes, and light shoes still beat everything that is not a shoe. No effect on your own category ladder, which never looked at shade.

Sanitizer

Switches off the two shoe rungs. Shoes become ordinary items ranked on shade alone, for everyone in the washer — and among your own items, shoes drop down to sit with clothing.

Coloring & catcher

As on the previous page: Coloring ruins every other player's laundry in the washer; a Color catcher saves its own owner from it and nobody else.

Sanitizer, and what it costs. A's dark shoes, B's light shirt, C's dark pants, Sanitizer by B → A's shoes and C's pants wash; B's shirt goes back. B played it and still lost their own shirt.

Bleach does not stop shoes. A's dark shoes and B's light shirt with a Bleach → the shoes still wash, the shirt still goes back. Bleach changes shade, never category.

Damp socks

Socks that wash in a washer that contained a blanket come out damp instead of clean.

Put them in your damp zone, face up. They are not clean yet. They need one more wash, anywhere — any washer, any day, with or without a blanket. Load them exactly as if they were in your hand. Once they wash again they are clean, even if that second wash is also beside a blanket.

The rule keys on the washer containing a blanket, not on the blanket washing. If a blanket is in there, socks that wash come out damp — end of story. Socks are the only item that is ever damp.

Winning

The moment a player's clean pile holds all ten of the items they were dealt, the game ends and they win. Victory is checked after the whole reckoning has finished, never in the middle of it. If two or more players complete their tenth item on the same day, they all win together. There is no tie-break.

The key

The keyholder goes first in the roll phase, and takes the key step each day, after the events and before the reckoning: switch one washer on, or one washer off, or pass. Not both. Not two. At the end of the day the key passes to the left.

Switching a washer off protects what is inside — nothing washes, but nothing comes back either, and it carries into tomorrow. Switching one on is how you recover from a Circuit break. Switching off a washer Jimothy is in does nothing at all. It is the single most valuable action of the day. Do not waste it.
