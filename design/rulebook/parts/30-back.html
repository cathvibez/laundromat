Glossary

Clean pile — where washed items go. Permanent. Ten ends the game.

Clothes — shoes, socks, pants, shirt, hat.

Crowding — three or more items of one type in one washer sending them all back. provisional — may be cut

Damp — a state only socks can be in, caused by washing beside a blanket. Needs one more wash.

Day — one full round: everyone rolls, the event happens, the key acts, the washers run.

Fresh — a special item card drawn today. Face up, unplayable until tomorrow.

Hostage — an item stuck in Jimothy's washer. Cannot wash, cannot move, released unwashed.

Item — any of the fourteen garment cards of one colour.

The key — the token that makes you go first and lets you switch one washer per day.

Linen — underwear and blanket.

Loading — putting an item from your hand or damp zone into a washer. Mandatory.

Ready — a special item card from a previous day. Playable.

The reckoning — step 4 of the day, when every washer that is on decides what washes.

The room — the other players' items in a washer, and the ladder they impose on yours.

Rung — a step of the room's ladder: dark shoes, light shoes, dark, light.

Sent back — returned to the owner's hand unwashed. No penalty.

Shade — dark or light. Not the same thing as your colour.

Washer — one of the machines. Holds four items. Also called a machine.

Shade must be readable across the table, in bad light, by a colour-blind player, and on the back-lit edge of a fanned hand. It cannot ride on tone alone: print the word DARK or LIGHT on the face, and repeat a shape mark that differs in silhouette — a solid disc versus an open ring — in the same corner on every card. Owner colour is a separate axis and must never be confusable with shade.

A full turn, played out

Four players: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow. Five washers. It is day 3, and Green holds the key.

Green goes first, because Green has the key. Green rolls a 2. Green has a Sanitizer ready since yesterday but nothing worth using it on, so no card. Green loads a dark shirt and a dark hat into washer 1, which is empty. Nothing else happens on a 2.

Yellow is next. Rolls a 5. Yellow plays a Mesh bag on washer 1 — before loading, which is the only time it works — then loads one item, a light underwear, into washer 1. That underwear is now in the bag. Then the 5: two cards off the top, a Snacc and a Bleach. Jimothy is not on the board, so Snacc would be dead weight; Yellow keeps the Bleach, which goes face up and cannot be played until tomorrow.

Red is next. Rolls a 6 — the first 6 today, so Red draws an event. It is Jimothy, turned face up at once. Red loads one item first, a light pants into washer 4. Then the event: Red chooses washer 3, which is holding two of Blue's items. Washer 3 will not run tonight and Blue's two items are hostages. (That timing is arm E1. Under E2 or E3, Jimothy would not settle until every player had finished their turn.)

Blue goes last, having held the key yesterday. Rolls a 4. Blue loads a dark sock into washer 1, then takes the move: Blue would love to pull the hostages out of washer 3, but hostages cannot be moved. Instead Blue lifts Green's dark hat out of washer 1 and drops it into washer 5, purely so that washer 1 has less dark in it.

Key step. Green looks at washer 2 — empty, on, useless — and at washer 1, which now holds Green's dark shirt, Yellow's bagged light underwear, and Blue's dark sock. Green leaves everything alone and passes.

Reckoning. Washer 3 does not run; Jimothy is in it. Washer 1 holds Green's dark shirt, Blue's dark socks, and Yellow's light underwear in a Mesh bag. Yellow's underwear: bagged — it washes, nothing else needs checking. Green's dark shirt: the highest rung any other player occupies is 3 (Blue's dark socks; Yellow's underwear is light, rung 4). The shirt is dark, rung 3, so it clears the room. It is Green's only item, so it is Green's top category. It washes. Blue's dark socks: rung 3 against rung 3. Clears. They wash — no blanket in there, so they are clean, not damp. Washer 4 holds Red's light pants alone: it washes.

End of day. Yellow's Bleach becomes ready. Nobody has ten clean items. The key passes from Green to Yellow, who will roll first tomorrow.

Rules that are not final

Everything marked with an amber provisional tag or fenced in an amber box, gathered in one place. Nothing on this list may go to print as it stands. Ordered by how much of the booklet each one changes.

1
The game's name — decide first.
A placeholder throughout: genericised former trademark, almost certainly unregistrable, and a published game already carries it. Suggestion on file: Jimothy's Laundromat. Changes the cover, every folio, three places in prose, and every asset outside this booklet. Rename before any art or layout money is spent.
2
When a drawn event resolves — E1 / E2 / E3.
Revealed on draw is settled; resolved on draw is not. Written for E1 — page 5, flagged again on page 21. Card faces are true under all three arms, so no card art is at risk. Nothing has been simulated. Decide this one first.
3
What Circuit break does — V1 / V2 / V3.
Three complete card faces are on page 16. Print one. The entry is written for V2; contents are retained under all three. Blocked behind item 2 — the existing V3 recommendation measured a world where a Coin could not answer the event.
4
How many of each special item card is in the 20-card deck.
The total is a manufacturing constraint; the split is unset. No count appears on any card. Blocked behind items 3 and 5.
5
The Mesh bag — keep, weaken, or revert; and its two sub-questions.
Comfortably the strongest card in the game, with no counterplay. Fallback “Wash net” text is intact on page 14. Sub-questions: do bagged items still set the ladder and count for crowding (yes), and do bagged socks beside a blanket still come out damp (yes). Changes page 10 line 7, page 14, and reckonings 11–12 on page 19.
6
Whether the crowding rule survives at all.
Fires in roughly one reckoning in a hundred, rarer still now that your own copies no longer crowd you. If cut, delete the crowding panel on page 10, line 5 of page 11, reckonings 5–6 on page 18, the back-page line, and the glossary entry. No card changes.
7
How the first keyholder is chosen.
Nothing in the design says, and nothing has been invented here. Page 4 step 5 carries the flag. One line of text is needed.
8
Whether damp socks get a physical marker.
The only lasting per-item state in the game, and the manifest has no token for it. Options: a punchboard token per player; the face-up damp zone alone; or the zone printed on a player mat, which adds a component. Page 3 lists the count as “?”.
9
Bleach's card wording — decided in substance, not signed off.
The shade-swap wording on page 13 is the rule as implemented; the brief's older shorthand gave the wrong answer whenever shoes were present, and has been corrected. Nothing to decide except whether you like the sentence.
One more, from outside this booklet
“Own items don't taint own items” — the rule this booklet's whole stage-one ladder is built on — is live and fully tested in the app but is not in the brief. Two consequences to sign off: a washer no longer resolves at a single tier, since each owner can be on a different rung at once (page 11's checklist is the mitigation, and it needs testing on a real table with real strangers); and underwear isolation and crowding are now partly redundant. The booklet cannot be finalised while its central mechanic is absent from the ground-truth document.

And one production note. Underwear, blanket, Gang, Circuit break and Animal control have no art. They appear here as hatched “art pending” plates, matching what the app renders, rather than as holes.

Quick reference
Laundromat one per player

The day

  1. Roll phase — keyholder first, then left.
  2. Event — if one was drawn today. provisional
  3. Key — keyholder turns one washer on, one off, or passes.
  4. Reckoning — every washer that is ON runs.
  5. End of day — fresh cards ready; key passes left.

Your turn

1. Roll.  2. Play one ready card (optional).  3. Load — mandatory.  4. The die's extra effect.

Setup

Washers = players + 1 · capacity 4 · deal 10 of your 14 · all start ON.

Winning

First to ten clean items. Checked after the whole reckoning. Ties win together.
