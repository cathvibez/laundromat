# LAUNDROMAT
### Rulebook

*3–6 players · ages 12+ · 45–135 minutes*

**Working title.** "Laundromat" is a placeholder. The name is expected to change before print — see
`open-questions-for-print.md` §1. Every other word here is final unless marked otherwise.

> **[UNRESOLVED] markers.** A handful of rules are still being decided. Wherever one appears in this
> rulebook it is marked in a box, with the options spelled out. **Nothing marked [UNRESOLVED] may go
> to print as it stands.** They are listed in one place in `open-questions-for-print.md`.

---

## 1. What you are doing

You have ten pieces of laundry. So does everyone else. There are not enough washers.

**The first player to get all ten of their items washed wins, and the game stops immediately.**

You cannot wash your laundry in private. Everybody shares the same handful of washers, and every
washer decides for itself, once a day, what comes out clean and what comes out untouched. A dark
sock thrown in at the last moment can ruin a whole machine for everybody. The whole board is face
up. Nothing is hidden except the cards in your hand — this is a puzzle you solve out loud, not a
bluffing game.

The rule that runs the whole game is one line long:

> ### Shoes first, then dark, then light.

Section 6 turns that line into the full rule. Read sections 1 to 5 first; they take five minutes.

## 2. Components

| Component | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Item cards | 84 | 6 colours × 14. One colour per player; unused colours stay in the box. |
| Special item cards | 20 | One shared deck. |
| Event cards | 4 | One shared deck: Gang, Circuit break, Jimothy, Animal control. |
| Washer tiles | 7 | Numbered 1–7. You will not use all of them at every player count. |
| On/off markers | 7 | One per washer. |
| Key token | 1 | |
| Jimothy | 1 | A raccoon. |
| Die | 1 | Six-sided. |
| Damp markers | ? | **[UNRESOLVED]** — see `open-questions-for-print.md` §8. |

### Your 14 item cards

Each colour holds one card of each of seven types, in each of two shades:

**Shoes · Socks · Pants · Shirt · Hat · Underwear · Blanket** — one **dark** and one **light** of
each.

A card of Socks is a pair, and counts as one item. Shoes, Socks, Pants, Shirt and Hat are
**clothes**. Underwear and Blanket are **linen**. Most rules say **item**, meaning any of the
fourteen.

[CALLOUT] **Shade is not owner colour.** Every colour has dark cards and light cards. "Dark" is
about how dirty the wash gets; your colour is about who the card belongs to.

## 3. Setup

1. **Pick colours.** Each player takes the 14 item cards of one colour. Return unused colours to the
   box.
2. **Set out the washers.** Use **one more washer than there are players**.

   | Players | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | Washers | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |

   Put an on/off marker on each, **set to ON**. Every washer starts on. Every washer holds up to
   **4 items**, at every player count.
3. **Deal your laundry.** Shuffle your own 14 cards, draw **10**, and take them into your hand.
   Put the other 4 back in the box **without looking at them**. Those four are not in the game.
   **The ten you drew are the ten you must wash.**
4. **Shuffle the two shared decks** — the 20 special item cards and the 4 event cards — and put them
   face down where everyone can reach them.
5. **Give someone the key.**

   > **[UNRESOLVED — first player.]** Nothing in the design says how the first keyholder is
   > chosen. Standard options: whoever did laundry most recently; youngest player; random. **The
   > designer must pick one line of text.**

6. Put Jimothy and the die to one side. You are ready.

[DIAGRAM: a 4-player setup from above — five numbered washer tiles in a row with ON markers, the
special and event decks beside them, four player areas each showing a hand of ten cards, an empty
clean pile and an empty damp zone, and the key in front of the first player.]

### Your play area

Three places, all in front of you:

- **Your hand** — the only private thing in the game.
- **Your damp zone** — face up, public. Damp socks live here (section 7.4). You may load from it
  exactly as if it were your hand.
- **Your clean pile** — face up. Washed items go here and never come back out. Ten cards here ends
  the game.

You will also have, later:

- **Fresh cards** — a special item card you drew today, face up where everyone can see it. It cannot
  be played today.
- **Ready cards** — special item cards from earlier days, held in hand and playable.

## 4. How a day works

The game is played in **days**. Every day has five steps, in this order:

1. **Roll phase.** Every player takes one turn.
2. **Event.** If an event card came up today, it happens.
3. **Key.** The keyholder switches one washer on, or one washer off, or does nothing.
4. **Reckoning.** Every washer that is ON runs. This is where laundry gets washed.
5. **End of day.** Fresh cards become ready. The key passes to the left.

### 4.1 Roll phase — acting order

**The keyholder goes first.** Play continues around the table to the left. The player sitting
immediately before the keyholder — that is, yesterday's keyholder — goes last.

Because the key passes every night, the order rotates with it. On day two the second player rolls
first, the first player goes last, and the second player also takes the key step.

### 4.2 Your turn, in four steps

Do these in order. You may not go back.

**Step 1 — Roll the die.**

| Roll | What you do |
|---|---|
| **1** | Load 1 item. |
| **2** | Load 2 items. |
| **3** | Load 3 items. |
| **4** | Load 1 item, then move any one item from one washer to another — including your own. |
| **5** | Load 1 item, then draw two special item cards and keep one. |
| **6** | Load 1 item, then draw an event card. |

**Step 2 — Play one special item card (optional).**

You may play **at most one** ready card, and only before you load. You may never play a card on the
day you drew it. See section 8.

**Step 3 — Load.**

Put exactly as many of your items into washers as you rolled. **Loading is not optional.** Section 5
covers where items may go, and what to do when the board will not take them.

**Step 4 — Resolve the die's extra effect**, if it has one — the move on a 4, the card draw on a 5,
the event on a 6.

[CALLOUT] **Cards before laundry.** The card comes first because the Mesh bag has to be open before
anything goes in it. Everything else about the order follows from that.

### 4.3 The die's extra effects in detail

**On a 4 — move one item.** After you have loaded, you may take any single item out of any washer
and put it into a different washer. It may be anybody's item, including your own. The destination
must be able to accept it under the normal loading rules (section 5). You may not move an item out
of a washer Jimothy is in, and destroyed washers are neither a source nor a destination. The move is
optional; the load is not.

**On a 5 — draw a special item.** Draw the top two cards of the special item deck, keep one, and put
the other on the **bottom** of the deck. Place the card you kept **face up** in front of you. It is
**fresh**: everyone can see it, and you cannot play it today. At the end of the day it becomes
**ready** and joins your hand.

**On a 6 — draw an event.** Only the **first 6 rolled each day** draws an event card. Any later 6
just loads an item. The card is turned **face up the instant it is drawn** — everyone sees what is
coming.

> **[UNRESOLVED — when the event actually happens.]** Three versions are live, and the day sequence
> in this rulebook is written for the first of them:
> - **E1 — immediate.** The event happens the moment it is drawn, in the middle of the drawer's
>   turn. Players who have not gone yet play into the changed board; players who have already gone
>   have no recourse. A Coin or a Snacc played later that same day can answer it.
> - **E2 — deferred.** The event is revealed on draw but does not happen until everybody has taken a
>   turn — step 2 of the day. Everyone loads under the same known threat. Nothing can answer it that
>   day.
> - **E3 — split.** Circuit break and Animal control happen on draw; Gang and Jimothy wait until
>   everyone has loaded.
>
> **This changes section 4's numbered list and nothing else.** Under E2 and E3 the deferred events
> resolve at day step 2, before the keyholder acts.

## 5. Loading

### 5.1 Where an item may go

A washer will take your item unless one of these is true:

- **It is full.** Four items is the limit, at every player count.
- **It has been destroyed** by the Gang.
- **Jimothy is in it.**
- **There is a blanket inside** and your item is not socks.
- **Your item is a blanket** and the washer holds anything other than socks.

That is the whole list. In particular:

[CALLOUT] **A washer that is switched OFF can still be loaded.** It just will not run. This matters
enormously after a Circuit break.

### 5.2 Blankets

A blanket needs a washer to itself — with one exception. **Socks may share with a blanket**, in
both directions: socks may be put into a washer holding a blanket, and a blanket may be put into a
washer holding nothing but socks. Nothing else may share with a blanket, ever.

Socks that wash beside a blanket come out **damp**. See section 7.4.

### 5.3 When you cannot load

You must load exactly the number you rolled. You load fewer only when you cannot:

- your hand and damp zone together hold fewer items than that, or
- no washer on the board will accept anything you hold.

Then you load as many as you legally can, **which may be none**. There is no penalty, no re-roll and
no substitute action. Say so out loud so the table can see it, and move on.

This is a real situation, not a technicality — after a Circuit break nothing drains and everyone
keeps loading.

## 6. The reckoning

This is the heart of the game. Read it once now and once after your first day.

**At the reckoning, every washer that is ON runs.** Each one decides, item by item, what comes out
clean and what comes back. Washers that are off, destroyed, or occupied by Jimothy do not run at all.

Everything that washes goes to its owner's clean pile, permanently. **Everything else goes straight
back to its owner's hand, with no penalty of any kind.** Nothing is ever lost, damaged or discarded.
After a washer has run, it is empty.

Washers are resolved one at a time and never affect each other. Item order inside a washer never
matters.

### 6.1 Stage one — your own laundry, on its own

Start with the simplest case: a washer holding only your items.

Among your own items, **shade does not matter**. What matters is the category:

> ### shoes → clothing and blankets → underwear

**Only your top category washes.** Everything below it goes back.

- Your own shoes, of either shade, stop your own shirt.
- Your own shirt stops your own underwear.
- Your own dark shirt does **not** stop your own light shirt — they are the same category and they
  wash together.

[EXAMPLE] You put your dark shirt, your light pants, your dark hat and your light socks in washer 3
and nobody else touches it. All four are clothing, all four are your top category, **all four wash.**

[EXAMPLE] You put your dark shoes and your light shirt in washer 3. Shoes are the higher category.
**The shoes wash. The shirt comes back.**

### 6.2 Stage two — what the room does to you

Now other players load into the same washer. **Other players' laundry is ranked by the full ladder,
and shade is back:**

> ### 1. dark shoes → 2. light shoes → 3. anything dark → 4. anything light

Find the highest rung that **another player's** item occupies in that washer. **Your item must be on
that rung or higher.** If it is lower, it goes back.

That is the whole cross-player rule. Your own other items are simply not consulted.

[EXAMPLE] You put in your light shirt. Another player puts in dark pants. Their dark pants sit on
rung 3; your light shirt is on rung 4, which is lower. **Your shirt comes back. Their pants wash.**

[EXAMPLE] You put in your light shirt and your dark pants. Another player puts in a light hat. The
highest rung an opponent occupies is 4. Your dark pants (rung 3) clear it; your light shirt (rung 4)
matches it; your own two items do not fight each other. **Both of yours wash.** Their hat is on rung
4 and your dark pants are on rung 3, above them. **Their hat comes back.**

[CALLOUT] **The two ladders in one line.** *The room ranks by shoes and shade. Your own pile ranks
only by category.* An item washes only if it survives both.

### 6.3 Stage three — the filters

An item that has cleared both ladders can still be sent back by one of these. Check them last.

**Underwear.** Underwear washes only in a washer holding nothing but underwear. Anything else in
there — anyone's, yours included — and your underwear comes back.

**Blankets.** A blanket must be alone, except that socks may share with it. Loading rules make this
almost impossible to break; if a washer somehow holds an illegal blanket mix, nothing in it washes.

**Crowding.** [UNRESOLVED — this rule may be cut entirely; see below.] If **other players** have put
two or more items of the same type as yours into that washer — three of a type in total, counting
yours — then yours is crowded out and comes back. Any colour, any shade; only the type matters. Your
own copies never crowd you.

**Coloring.** If another player has played a Coloring card on the washer, your items there are
ruined and come back — unless you have a Color catcher on that washer.

> **[UNRESOLVED — crowding.]** The crowding rule fires in about one reckoning in a hundred and the
> designer has not decided whether it earns its rules text. If it is cut, delete the crowding
> paragraph above, the crowding line from the quick reference, and worked examples 5 and 6.

### 6.4 The full check, in order

For each item in a washer that is running, its owner asks:

1. **The room.** Is it on the highest rung any *other player's* item occupies here, or higher?
   *(dark shoes → light shoes → dark → light)*
2. **Your pile.** Is it in your top category here? *(shoes → clothing and blankets → underwear)*
3. **Underwear.** If it is underwear, is everything else in the washer also underwear?
4. **Blanket.** If a blanket is here, is it alone but for socks?
5. **Crowding.** Do other players have two or more of the same type here?
6. **Coloring.** Is there an opponent's Coloring here that you cannot catch?

All clear → **it washes**. Any one of them fails → **it goes back**.

And overriding every line above:

7. **The Mesh bag.** Anything in a Mesh bag washes, full stop.

## 7. The rest of the reckoning

### 7.1 What the cards do to it

Three special item cards attach to a washer and are read when it runs:

- **Bleach** swaps every shade in the washer before the room's ladder is read. Dark counts as light,
  light counts as dark. It does not disarm shoes — bleached dark shoes are light shoes, and light
  shoes still beat everything that is not a shoe. It has no effect at all on your own category
  ladder, which never looked at shade.
- **Sanitizer** switches off the two shoe rungs. Shoes become ordinary items ranked on shade alone,
  for everyone in the washer — and among your own items, shoes drop down to sit with clothing.
- **Coloring** and **Color catcher** work as described in section 6.3.

### 7.2 Machines that do not run

| State | Runs? | Can be loaded? | How it got there | How it gets out |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ON** | yes | yes | the default | key, Coin, Circuit break |
| **OFF** | no — **keeps everything inside** | **yes** | key, Coin, Circuit break | key, Coin |
| **Jimothy** | no — contents held hostage | no | the Jimothy event | Snacc, Animal control |
| **Destroyed** | never again | no | the Gang event | never |

An OFF washer carries its contents into tomorrow untouched. Everything loaded into it just piles up.

### 7.3 Everything goes back to the owner

Items that do not wash go straight back to their owner's hand. Nothing is discarded and nothing is
locked out. The only thing you lose is the day.

### 7.4 Damp socks

**Socks that wash in a washer that contained a blanket come out damp instead of clean.** Put them in
your damp zone, face up. They are not clean yet.

Damp socks need **one more wash, anywhere** — any washer, any day, with or without a blanket. Load
them exactly as if they were in your hand. Once they wash again they are clean, even if that second
wash is also beside a blanket.

The rule keys on the washer **containing** a blanket, not on the blanket itself washing. If a blanket
is in there, socks that wash come out damp — end of story.

Socks are the only item that is ever damp.

## 8. Special item cards

Twenty cards, seven kinds, one shared deck. You get them by rolling a 5.

**Drawing.** Draw two, keep one, put the other on the bottom of the deck. The card you keep sits
**face up** in front of you and cannot be played today.

**Playing.** On your turn, before you load, you may play **one** ready card. Everything a card does
either happens immediately (Coin, Snacc) or attaches to a washer and waits for the reckoning
(Coloring, Color catcher, Bleach, Mesh bag, Sanitizer). An attached card does its job once, when
that washer runs, and then goes back into the deck and is shuffled in. A card at a washer that never
runs — because it was switched off, or was empty — is simply wasted.

| Card | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Coloring** | Play on a washer. When it runs, every other player's laundry inside is ruined and goes back. |
| **Color catcher** | Play on a washer. Your laundry inside is safe from Coloring. Nothing else. |
| **Bleach** | Play on a washer. Every item inside counts as the opposite shade. Shoes are still shoes. |
| **Mesh bag** | Play on a washer as you load. Everything you load into that washer this turn washes when it runs, whatever else is inside. |
| **Sanitizer** | Play on a washer. Shoes count as ordinary clothes there. |
| **Coin** | Turn any one washer on or off, immediately. You do not need the key. One use. |
| **Snacc** | Move Jimothy to another washer. Whatever he was sitting on goes back to its owners, unwashed. |

Full text and edge cases for each card: `card-text.md`. Three points that catch people out:

- **The Mesh bag covers only what you load this turn, into that one washer.** Items already sitting
  in the washer from yesterday are not in the bag.
- **Bagged items are still in the washer for everyone else.** Your bagged dark shoes wash for
  certain *and* still wreck the wash for the other players.
- **The Coin is not a second key.** It is spent the moment it is played, and the keyholder acts after
  every player has taken their turn, so the keyholder can undo it the same day.

## 9. Events

Four cards, one of each. The first 6 rolled each day draws one; later 6s do not. At most one event
happens per day. The card is turned face up the instant it is drawn.

**Gang.** The drawer picks a washer. It is destroyed permanently — its contents go back to their
owners and it is out of the game, leaving the board one washer smaller for good. Gang happens once
and never returns to the deck. If the destroyed washer is Jimothy's, the hostages are released and
Jimothy relocates to another washer, chosen by the same player.

**Circuit break.** [UNRESOLVED — one of three; see section 4.3's box and
`open-questions-for-print.md` §3.] As written in the brief: every surviving washer switches off and
keeps its contents. The keyholder can bring one back per day. Off washers can still be loaded, and
loading is still mandatory, so the board fills up while nothing drains. This is the most dangerous
card in the game.

**Jimothy.** The drawer picks a washer; a raccoon moves in. It cannot run and cannot be loaded, and
everything already inside is a hostage — frozen, unmovable even by a roll of 4, and released only
when he leaves. He leaves only by **Snacc** or **Animal control**. Gang and Circuit break do not
touch him. Release never washes anything.

**Animal control.** Jimothy is removed and his hostages go back to their owners, unwashed. If
Jimothy is not on the board, nothing happens — which is most of the time.

Circuit break, Animal control and Jimothy's card go back into the event deck after they have done
their work. Gang does not.

---

## 10. The key

The keyholder does two things:

1. **Goes first** in the roll phase.
2. **Takes the key step** each day, after the events and before the reckoning: switch **one** washer
   on, or **one** washer off, or pass. Not both. Not two.

At the end of the day the key passes to the player on the left.

[CALLOUT] Switching a washer **off** protects whatever is inside it — nothing washes, but nothing
comes back either, and it carries into tomorrow. Switching one **on** is how you recover from a
Circuit break. Putting a washer that Jimothy is in into the "off" state does nothing at all; it
already could not run. It is the single most valuable action of the day. Do not waste it.

---

## 11. Winning

**The moment a player's clean pile holds all ten of the items they were dealt, the game ends and
they win.**

Victory is checked **after the whole reckoning has finished**, never in the middle of it. If two or
more players complete their tenth item on the same day, **they all win together**. There is no
tie-break.

## 12. A full turn, played out

*Four players: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow. Five washers. It is day 3, and Green holds the key.*

**Green goes first, because Green has the key.** Green rolls a **2**. Green has a Sanitizer that has
been ready since yesterday, but nothing worth using it on yet, so no card. Green loads a dark shirt
and a dark hat into washer 1, which is empty. Nothing else happens on a 2.

**Yellow is next.** Yellow rolls a **5**. Yellow plays a **Mesh bag** on washer 1 — *before* loading,
which is the only time it works — then loads one item, a light underwear, into washer 1. That
underwear is now in the bag. Yellow then resolves the 5: two special item cards off the top, a Snacc
and a Bleach. Jimothy is not on the board, so Snacc would be dead weight; Yellow keeps the **Bleach**
and puts the Snacc on the bottom of the deck. The Bleach goes face up in front of Yellow and cannot
be played until tomorrow.

**Red is next.** Red rolls a **6** — the first 6 today, so Red draws an event. It is **Jimothy**, and
it is turned face up at once. Red loads one item first: a light pants into washer 4. Then the event.
Red chooses washer 3, which is holding two of Blue's items. Jimothy settles in. Washer 3 will not run
tonight and Blue's two items are hostages.

*(That timing is arm E1. Under E2 or E3, Jimothy would not settle until every player had finished
their turn.)*

**Blue goes last, having held the key yesterday.** Blue rolls a **4**. Blue loads a dark sock into
washer 1, then takes the move: Blue would love to pull the hostages out of washer 3, but hostages
cannot be moved. Instead Blue lifts Green's dark hat out of washer 1 and drops it into washer 5,
purely so that washer 1 has less dark in it.

**Key step.** Green, still the keyholder, looks at washer 2 — empty, on, useless — and at washer 1.
Washer 1 now holds Green's dark shirt, Yellow's bagged light underwear, and Blue's dark sock. Green
leaves everything alone and passes.

**Reckoning.** Washer 3 does not run; Jimothy is in it. The others do.

Washer 1 holds: Green's dark shirt, Blue's dark socks, Yellow's light underwear **in a Mesh bag**.

- *Yellow's underwear:* bagged. It washes. Nothing else needs checking.
- *Green's dark shirt:* the highest rung any other player occupies is rung 3 — Blue's dark socks, and
  Yellow's underwear is light, rung 4. The shirt is dark, rung 3, so it clears the room. It is
  Green's only item, so it is Green's top category. No blanket, no crowd, no Coloring. **It washes.**
- *Blue's dark socks:* rung 3 against Green's rung 3. Clears. **They wash.** No blanket in there, so
  they are clean, not damp.

Washer 4 holds Red's light pants alone: nobody else is in there and it is Red's only item. **It
washes.**

**End of day.** Yellow's Bleach becomes ready. Nobody has ten clean items. The key passes from Green
to Yellow, who will roll first tomorrow.

## 13. Tricky reckonings

Every one of these is a real board that has come up. *"A's dark shoes"* means the item belongs to
player A.

**1 — Shoes beat everything, including other dark items.**
Washer holds A's dark shoes, B's light shirt, C's dark pants.
→ **A's dark shoes wash. Everything else goes back.** Dark shoes are the top rung of the room's
ladder. C's dark pants are on rung 3 and lose to it, even though they are dark.

**2 — Your own two shirts are friends. An opponent's shirt is not.**
Washer holds A's dark shirt, A's light shirt, B's dark shirt.
→ **Only A's dark shirt washes.** A's light shirt is on rung 4 and B's dark shirt sits on rung 3
above it, so the room sends it back — your own items never protect you from someone else's. B's dark
shirt clears the room, but there are now three shirts in the washer and two of them are A's, so
**crowding** takes it.
*(Under the older rule where your own items tainted each other, all three went back.)*

**3 — Underwear is the hardest thing in the game to wash.**
Washer holds A's dark underwear and A's dark shirt.
→ **The shirt washes. The underwear goes back.** Twice over: clothing outranks underwear on A's own
ladder, and underwear only ever washes in a washer holding nothing but underwear. It is your own
laundry that ruined it. Nobody did this to you.

**4 — Underwear alone is fine, and shade still bites across players.**
A's dark underwear and A's light underwear, nothing else → **both wash**: same category, shade is
irrelevant among your own items.
A's dark underwear and B's light underwear → **only A's washes.** B is another player, so the room's
ladder applies and dark outranks light.

**5 — Three shoes cancel each other out.**
Washer holds A's dark shoes, B's dark shoes, C's dark shoes and D's light shirt. It is full.
→ **Nothing washes at all.** All three pairs of shoes clear the room's ladder, and then crowding
sends all three back. D's light shirt is not rescued: crowding sends items back, it never promotes
anything.

**6 — A blanket, three pairs of socks, and nobody happy.**
Washer holds A's dark blanket, A's dark socks, B's light socks, C's dark socks. Legal, and exactly
full.
→ **Only the blanket washes.** Each pair of socks sees the other two beside it, which is three socks,
so crowding takes all three. The blanket only has two socks beside it, which is not a crowd, and
nothing outranks it. Since no socks washed, nobody even gets a damp partial.

**7 — Damp socks, and the reading that surprises people.**
Washer holds A's light blanket and A's dark socks.
→ **The blanket washes and the socks come out damp.** Both are A's, both are in A's top category, and
nobody else is in the washer, so both would have washed — but socks that wash beside a blanket are
never clean the first time. Put them in the damp zone; one more wash anywhere finishes them.
Note that this holds even when the blanket does *not* wash: the rule is about the blanket being in
there, not about the blanket getting clean.

**8 — Sanitizer, and what it costs.**
Washer holds A's dark shoes, B's light shirt, C's dark pants, and B has played a **Sanitizer**.
→ **A's dark shoes and C's dark pants both wash. B's light shirt goes back.** With the shoe rungs
switched off, the washer is decided on shade alone, and the shoes are just another dark item.
Compare example 1: the same washer, one card, two washes instead of one. B played the card and still
lost their own shirt — Sanitizer helps whoever it happens to help.

**9 — Bleach does not stop shoes.**
Washer holds A's dark shoes and B's light shirt, with a **Bleach** on it.
→ **A's dark shoes still wash and B's shirt still goes back.** Bleach swaps shades, so the shoes read
as light shoes and the shirt reads as dark — but shoes still outrank everything that is not a shoe.
Bleach changes shade, never category.

**10 — Coloring, and the one player who ducks it.**
Washer holds A's light shirt, B's light pants, C's light hat, with a **Coloring** played by A and a
**Color catcher** played by B.
→ **A and B wash. C goes back.** Everything is light, so the ladder would have washed all three;
Coloring ruins everyone but A, and B's catcher saves B. The catcher protects its owner and nobody
else.

**11 — The Mesh bag beats the ladder but does not hide from it.**
Washer holds A's dark shoes **in a Mesh bag** and B's light shirt.
→ **A's shoes wash — they were always going to — and B's shirt still goes back.** The bag guarantees
its own contents and changes nothing for anyone else. The shoes are still dark shoes as far as the
room is concerned.

**12 — The Mesh bag does not reach backwards.**
A's underwear has been sitting in a switched-off washer since yesterday. Today A plays a Mesh bag on
that washer, loads nothing into it, and it is switched back on. Also inside: B's light shirt.
→ **Both go back.** The bag covers what you load *on the turn you play it*. Yesterday's underwear was
already in there and is not in the bag, so underwear isolation takes it as usual.

---

## 14. Odds and ends

**Two washers cannot affect each other.** Resolve them in any order you like.

**Order inside a washer never matters.** There is no "first in, first out". Every item is judged
against the same contents.

**A special item card played at a washer that never runs is wasted** — switched off, empty, or
destroyed, it makes no difference. The card still returns to the deck.

**Switching off a washer Jimothy is in does nothing.** It already could not run, and you have spent
the key on it.

**Animal control does not save your laundry.** Hostages come back unwashed. Every player expects
otherwise once.

**A Coin can be overruled.** It resolves during your turn; the keyholder acts later the same day.

**Everything on the floor is public.** Machine contents, damp zones, clean piles, fresh cards, on/off
markers, Jimothy, the wreck the Gang left. Only your hand and your ready cards are yours alone.

**Nothing is ever destroyed except washers.** No item card ever leaves the game once it is dealt.

## 15. Quick reference

*Print this page as a player aid, one per player.*

### The day

1. **Roll phase** — keyholder first, then to the left.
2. **Event** — if one was drawn today.
3. **Key** — the keyholder turns one washer on, one off, or passes.
4. **Reckoning** — every washer that is ON runs.
5. **End of day** — fresh cards become ready; the key passes left.

### Your turn

1. Roll. 2. Play one ready card (optional). 3. **Load — mandatory.** 4. The die's extra effect.

### The die

| 1 | Load 1 |
| 2 | Load 2 |
| 3 | Load 3 |
| 4 | Load 1 + move any one item between washers |
| 5 | Load 1 + draw two special items, keep one |
| 6 | Load 1 + draw an event (first 6 of the day only) |

Load fewer than you rolled only if your hand or the board will not allow it.

### The reckoning — for each item, in order

| | Check | Sent back if |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The room** — dark shoes → light shoes → dark → light | another player has something on a higher rung |
| 2 | **Your own pile** — shoes → clothing & blanket → underwear (shade ignored) | you have something of yours in a higher category |
| 3 | **Underwear** | anything in the washer is not underwear |
| 4 | **Blanket** | the blanket is sharing with anything but socks |
| 5 | **Crowding** | other players have 2+ of the same type here |
| 6 | **Coloring** | an opponent's Coloring is here and you have no catcher |
| — | **Mesh bag** | never. Bagged items always wash. |

Socks that wash beside a blanket come out **damp** — one more wash, anywhere, finishes them.

### Washer states

**ON** runs, loadable · **OFF** does not run, keeps contents, **still loadable** · **Jimothy** dead,
contents hostage · **Destroyed** gone for good.

### Setup at a glance

Washers = players + 1 · capacity 4 · deal 10 of your 14 · all washers start ON.

### Winning

First to ten clean items. Checked after the whole reckoning. Ties win together.

## 16. Glossary

**Clean pile** — where washed items go. Permanent. Ten ends the game.

**Clothes** — shoes, socks, pants, shirt, hat.

**Crowding** — three or more items of one type in one washer sending them all back. *[UNRESOLVED —
may be cut.]*

**Damp** — a state only socks can be in, caused by washing beside a blanket. Needs one more wash.

**Day** — one full round: everyone rolls, the event happens, the key acts, the washers run.

**Fresh** — a special item card drawn today. Face up, unplayable until tomorrow.

**Hostage** — an item stuck in Jimothy's washer. Cannot wash, cannot move, released unwashed.

**Item** — any of the fourteen garment cards of one colour.

**The key** — the token that makes you go first and lets you switch one washer per day.

**Linen** — underwear and blanket.

**Loading** — putting an item from your hand or damp zone into a washer. Mandatory.

**Ready** — a special item card from a previous day. Playable.

**The reckoning** — step 4 of the day, when every washer that is on decides what washes.

**The room** — the other players' items in a washer, and the ladder they impose on yours.

**Rung** — a step of the room's ladder: dark shoes, light shoes, dark, light.

**Sent back** — returned to the owner's hand unwashed. No penalty.

**Shade** — dark or light. Not the same thing as your colour.

**Washer** — one of the machines. Holds four items. Also called a machine.
